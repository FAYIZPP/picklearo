Our Story

From Kerala's Heart,
To Your Table

PICKLEARO was born in 2020 in the lush green heart of Kerala — Muttipalam, Manjeri — where generations of our family have turned the season's freshest organic produce into jars of tangy perfection. Those traditional recipes became the foundation of everything we do.

We partner directly with local Kerala farmers who share our obsession with quality and organic farming. Every ingredient is hand-selected, every spice blend mixed by hand, and every jar sealed with the same care our family gave their very first batch.

What started as a home kitchen passion is now loved across Kerala and beyond — with 30+ varieties, FSSAI certification, and delivery available. But we still make every jar the old-fashioned way, because some things should never change.
