The 21-Day Sun Craft

Why Factory Pickles Can Never Match Traditional Sunshine Pickling

Commercial pickles are made in stainless-steel vats with synthetic acetic acid (vinegar) in 48 hours. At Picklearo, we honor the ancient wisdom of slow-batch pickling.

01

Hand-Selected Seasonal Produce

Firm Ramkela & Totapuri mangoes, mountain garlic pods, and thick Guntur chilies picked at peak ripeness.

02

Freshly Stone-Ground Whole Spices

Fenugreek, yellow mustard, turmeric, and sea salt roasted in iron pans and coarse-pounded.

03

21 Days of Direct Sun Curing

Covered with muslin cloth and placed on traditional terrace sun-beds to mature naturally under radiant sunlight.

04

Cold-Pressed Oil Sealing

Topped with virgin wood-pressed sesame or mustard oil to preserve crispness naturally for over 12 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of Picklearo pickles?
Our pickles have a natural shelf life of 12 months from the manufacturing date when stored in a cool, dry place. Because we rely on high quality cold-pressed oil and roasted spices as natural preservatives, ensure you always use a clean, dry spoon!
Do you use any vinegar, chemical preservatives, or artificial colors?
Never. We use zero sodium benzoate, zero glacial acetic acid (industrial vinegar), and zero artificial food colors. All tartness comes from raw green mangoes, lemons, tamarind, or natural fermentation.
How are the pickles packaged to prevent transit leaks?
Every jar is packed in high-grade food-safe glass or ceramic jars sealed with induction foil and cushioned inside biodegradable honeycomb shockproof mailer boxes. We guarantee 100% leak-free delivery or an instant free replacement.
